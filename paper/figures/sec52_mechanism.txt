the quantity that decides transfer: is the tolerance MASKED here?
================================================================================================
  A threshold transfers only if the ratio e_tol/e_discr keeps the same
  side of 1 at both discretisations.  That ratio is what we tabulate.

     400   0.0025    5.429e-09     2.339e-09     5.494e-12

  A ratio that crosses 1 as dt changes is a threshold that does not
  transfer.  A ratio that stays on one side is one that does.
