**The far-field dependence.** In the confirmation map (residualControl 10⁻⁶), L/D rises by
about 9 % from R = 50 to 500 and changes by −0.1 to +2 % from 500 to 5,000 (the R = 5,000
values carry the iterative motion of 0.2–1 % found there). A fit L/D = Q + A/R to R = 50 and
500 gives A ≈ −385 to −392 on every mesh of m 4 %, t 12 % (e.g. n_surf 256, dy1 4×10⁻⁵:
74.76, 81.80 → Q = 82.58, predicted 82.50 at R = 5,000, measured 82.57). By that fit the
truncation error at R = 5,000 is |A| / 5,000 ≈ 0.08 in L/D, about 0.1 % — an inference from
the coarse meshes, not measured on the reference mesh. The R = 50,000 run (77.96, drifting)
lies 3 % below the R = 5,000 one, the opposite of the fit's direction: it is the unsettled
value, not the far-field limit.
