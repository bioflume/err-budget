## Two findings that explain it

**1. At R = 5,000, residualControl does not converge L/D, and the linear-solver tolerance
sets where it stops.** Relative change of L/D (residualControl 10⁻⁶) over the 15 meshes of
each radius:

| GAMG tolerance | R = 50 | R = 500 | R = 5,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10⁻⁶ → 10⁻⁷ | median 0.01 % (max 0.04 %) | 0.30 % (5.5 %) | 24 % (90 %) |
| 10⁻⁷ → 10⁻⁸ | 0.00 % (0.01 %) | 0.02 % (0.12 %) | 3.6 % (14.5 %) |

At the end of the runs the ω equation is not being solved: its normalised initial residual
sits just below its tolerance (gamg_tol × 0.01) and the smoother does zero iterations: in (c)
9.07×10⁻¹¹ against 10⁻¹⁰, rising slowly; in the settled reference 9.97×10⁻¹² against 10⁻¹¹.
*Inference:* the residuals are normalised over a domain that the far field dominates, so at
R = 5,000 neither residualControl nor the tolerance controls the near-wall turbulence, and
L/D ends where the iteration froze. Consistent with this: (c) (n_surf 256, dy1 10⁻⁵) lies
2.5 % above the settled n_surf 362 run at the same dy1 (51.40), where one mesh step at
dy1 5×10⁻⁶ is 0.33 %; its aft upper-surface skin friction is lower than at R = 500 (C_f at
x = 0.95: 1.51 against 1.75 ×10⁻³), unlike the settled runs, where R = 500 and 5,000 agree.
Not measured directly: no settled run of (c) exists.

**2. The first-cell height carries about 2 % per halving and does not converge on the
ladder.** dy1 2×10⁻⁵ → 10⁻⁵ changes L/D by −1.9 to −2.3 % at R = 50 and 500 on every mesh,
and 10⁻⁵ → 5×10⁻⁶ by −2.0 % on the settled n_surf 362 runs at R = 5,000 (mean y⁺ 0.64 →
0.32). It is drag (C_d +2.0 %; C_l 0.437–0.440 throughout): the turbulent skin friction rises
as dy1 falls. At R = 5,000 the grid hides it (2×10⁻⁵ → 10⁻⁵: −0.85 % to +0.03 % on n_surf
128–256), which is why the fine-mesh starts certify dy1 2×10⁻⁵ (inference). The reference
is therefore itself uncertain at about the 2 % level in dy1 (inference: the next halving is
unmeasured).

## Why errbudget accepted at the eight fine-mesh starts

Replayed from n_surf 181, dy1 4×10⁻⁵, R 5,000, residualControl 10⁻⁵, GAMG 10⁻⁸ (56 runs):
it accepts n_surf 256, dy1 2×10⁻⁵, R 5,000, residualControl 10⁻⁶, GAMG 10⁻⁸ with an error
estimate of 0.053 in L/D (0.1 %; target 0.504). Along each parameter at that setting:

| parameter | L/D, coarse → fine | budget entry r_k |
|---|---|---|
| GAMG tolerance | 43.60, 56.10, 52.65 | −9×10⁻¹² |
| residualControl | 31.38, 51.71, 52.71, 52.65 | −0.0019 |
| dy1 | 52.10, 52.65, 52.67 | −0.017 |
| radius | 50.69, 52.46, 52.65 | −0.023 |
| n_surf | 51.33, 52.87, 53.00, 52.85, 52.65 | −0.0003 |

Along the GAMG tolerance the changes oscillate (−9.05 and +3.45 from the finest value), and
the fit through the current run puts the remaining error at zero; iterative-solver
parameters are not order-checked (article, Section 3.2), and the tolerance is at the finest
value of its list. The dy1 changes (0.55, then 0.016) look converged at R = 5,000, unlike
at R = 50 and 500 and in the settled runs (finding 2).
