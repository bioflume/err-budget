## Two failure modes

1. **Start-up** (the reference meshes, four n 256 meshes, one n 64 at R 5000): the impulsive
   start from uniform flow with SIMPLEC and U relaxation 0.9 fails within ~150 iterations on
   the largest meshes, or settles in a wrong state. Design-independent in kind; needs a
   start-up procedure (e.g. potential-flow initial fields, or a first stage with lower
   relaxation).
2. **Limit cycles** of the thick cambered sections (separation over the last few per cent of
   the upper surface at 4°): on 12–20 of 45 meshes the iteration does not converge, and L/D
   varies by about 1 %. Capping the camber at 4.5 % leaves 13 (t 16) or 6 (t 14) of 45 meshes
   unconverged.
